About the Lecture
About the Lecture

Stopping the Pendulum - A Lecture That Changes Reality

The lecture Stopping the Pendulum was born from the book series by Nadav Teller and Teller Books Publishing: "The Secular God", "Dual State Theory", "The Civilizational Witness Scale", and "Tao Stories for Children".

The social pendulum is a natural movement that drives the person, society, and state when they are not aware of themselves.

The lecture brings forward the concept of metacognition, the human ability to think about thinking, and the concept of witnessing, the ability to witness emotions, thoughts, impulses, and desires without tying identity to them.

Through the tool of silence, the lecture shows that every person, society, or state can stop the vortex of life and find true inner balance.

The lecture is based on known philosophies from around the world, including Taoism, Stoicism, Buddhism, Jewish Kabbalah, Zen, effortless action, finding one's true purpose, and the philosophy of witnessing.

It teaches the audience that behind the noise of life, behind phenomena, behind social media, institutional media, social customs, and unripe desires, there is a real silence: a silence that leads the person, society, and state toward true purpose, natural synchronization with life, and flow without resistance to the phenomena of life.

About Nadav Teller

Nadav Teller

Nadav Teller is an Israeli author, educator, philosopher, jurist, and researcher of the state. For more than a decade he has worked with children, students, and adults, while researching the systems that shape human beings and societies.

His path combines law, political thought, yoga, tai chi, qi gong, meditation, and years of spiritual inquiry. Instead of separating the inner world of the person from public life, Teller examines how consciousness, institutions, technology, and power influence one another.

He is the creator of "The Secular God", "Dual State Theory", "The Civilizational Witness Scale", "Book of Foundation", and "Tao Stories for Children". At the foundation of his works are witness consciousness, inner stability, social balance, and the future of humanity. His books were written for readers who seek serious ideas in a clear and accessible language.

At the heart of his writing stands one question: how can human beings become more aware, responsible, and free, and build systems worthy of that development?
