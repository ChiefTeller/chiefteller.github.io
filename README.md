## Core Message

**Stopping the Pendulum** is a foundational lecture about the personal, social, and state pendulum:

- The personal pendulum: from reaction to witnessing.
- The social pendulum: from polarization to unity.
- The state pendulum: from chaos to order.

The site emphasizes lectures in many forms: interesting lectures, inspirational lectures, book lectures, spiritual lectures, political science lectures, lectures for organizations, lectures for companies, home salon lectures, and public lectures.

The lecture world connects books, spirit, spirituality, silence, Taoism, Stoicism, political science, capitalism, socialism, society, consciousness, redemption, witness consciousness, metacognition, social polarization, inner balance, and the possibility of beginning again.
