## Featured Book: The Secular God / האלוהים החילוני

The Secular God / האלוהים החילוני is a spiritual and philosophical book by Nadav Teller.

The book addresses curious seekers and people who feel that reality contains something deeper than noise, fear, ego and daily struggle. It does not try to convert readers, prove God or force religious language. It offers a secular, human and accessible gateway to the divine through silence, witness consciousness, humility, compassion, breath, acceptance and releasing resistance to life.
